## 4. Revenue Split — Gifts and Tips (Default)

4.1 **Default gift split (Fair rates model):**

- **Creator receives: 70%** of the gift or tip amount successfully collected by the Platform (net of non-refundable processor failures).  
- **Platform retains: 30%** as a service, hosting, checkout, and operations commission.

4.2 Example: A fan sends a **£10** gift.

- Creator share: **£7.00**  
- Platform share: **£3.00**  

4.3 Gift amounts shown to fans are **gross face value** before the split. Creator dashboards / session end notices should reflect Creator share where enabled.

4.4 Chargebacks, refunds, fraud reversals, and payment failures may reduce or reverse Creator balances. We may withhold or claw back amounts reasonably related to those events.

4.5 Promo windows (for example a temporary 0% Platform cut) apply only when **expressly published** for a defined period and region. After the promo ends, the default split in section 4.1 applies.
